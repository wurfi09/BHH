Privacy Policy – BHH Bot

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Discord bot "BHH Bot" (the "Bot"), operated by wurfi (the "Operator"), processes and how that data may be used. By using the Bot, you agree to this Policy.

1. Data the Bot processes

  • Team statistics: player names, player IDs and weekly KMS values of BHH members, read from a team-internal spreadsheet maintained by BHH.
  • Commands: the content of messages that start with the Bot's command prefix (for example .kms or .top), only to respond to them.

The Bot does not store, log, or save messages, Discord user data, or any other personal data. It reads the team spreadsheet when a command is used and replies in the channel where the command was sent.

2. Use of data – team channels only

All data provided by the Bot is intended exclusively for use within BHH team channels.

  • Data from the Bot may only be used and displayed in BHH team channels.
  • Data from the Bot must not be shared with anyone outside the team – this includes screenshots, copies, forwarding, or posting it in other servers, channels, or platforms.

Users who share Bot data outside the team violate this Policy and the Terms of Service and may lose access to the Bot.

3. Data sharing by the Operator

The Operator does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is only processed through Discord as required to deliver the Bot's responses.

4. Data storage and security

Team statistics are kept in a spreadsheet managed by BHH. The Bot itself has no database and keeps no copies of the data. Reasonable care is taken to keep the spreadsheet and the Bot's access credentials secure.

5. Your rights

If you are a BHH member and want your data corrected or removed from the team statistics, contact the Operator (see Section 7).

6. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Bot after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact wurfi directly on Discord. To add wurfi as a friend, use the Discord username wurf8288.
